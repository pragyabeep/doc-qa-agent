# Billing and Refunds

## Refund policy

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Monthly plans do not receive partial-month refunds; access continues until the end of the paid month.

Annual plans receive a full refund if you cancel within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refunded. Instead you receive a prorated account credit for the unused months.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Invoices

Find invoices under Settings, then Billing, then Invoices. Invoices can be downloaded as PDF. Business plan admins can add a company tax ID to invoices.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, CloudSync retries on day 1, day 3 and day 7. If the payment still fails, the account becomes read-only after day 14. Files stay safe and downloadable while the account is read-only. Updating the payment method restores full access immediately.

## Adding seats mid-cycle

Seats added in the middle of a billing cycle are charged on a prorated basis for the remaining days of the cycle.
