# Security and Single Sign-On

## Encryption

Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and with TLS 1.3 in transit.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is available on all plans. It supports authenticator apps (TOTP) and hardware security keys.

## Single sign-on and provisioning

SAML 2.0 single sign-on is available on the Business plan only. SCIM user provisioning is also Business plan only.

## Data residency

Business plan customers can choose to store data in the US or in the EU (Frankfurt). Free and Pro plans store data in the US.

## Deleted files and trash

Deleted files stay in the trash and can be restored for 7 days on Free, 30 days on Pro, and 90 days on Business. After that they are permanently deleted.

## Audit logs

Audit logs record sign-ins, sharing changes and admin actions. They are available on the Business plan only and are kept for 12 months.
